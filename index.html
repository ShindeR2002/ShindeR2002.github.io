Data-Driven Analysis of Inflatable Space Structures

Built a full ML pipeline for predicting wrinkling onset and stiffness degradation in Kapton inflatable booms used in space deployable structures. Integrated experimental cantilever testing (Arduino DAQ + stepper motor), image processing via OpenCV for rotation measurement, and FEM simulations in LS-Dyna — then trained regression and ensemble models cross-validated against simulation results.
